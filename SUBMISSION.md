# Badzone Run: Versus

Play: https://badzone-run.onrender.com

## Description

Two drivers. Two AI rivals. Three hazardous stretches of industrial wasteland. Badzone Run: Versus turns tabletop racing decisions into a shared browser battle: pick a scrap-built chassis, preview your route, manage speed, and risk shooting or ramming your opponent. Server-resolved dice turn careful plans into skids, wrecks and narrow escapes. Create a private room, share its code and race a friend—no accounts needed. Original vehicle art, distinct hazards and a full Solo mode complete the package.

## How to test

1. Open the link in two independent browsers/devices.
2. Player 1 chooses a chassis, creates a room, and shares the code.
3. Player 2 chooses a chassis and joins with that code.
4. Both mark ready. Only the current driver can confirm a maneuver.
5. Complete the race, then both request Rematch and ready up again.

The first connection after the free server idles may take a minute. Refreshing the same tab reconnects to its seat. Server restarts clear active rooms. Solo is available without a second player. This document is submission material; no contest submission has been sent.
