**Title:** Badzone Run

**Description (under 500 characters):**

Race. Wreck. Upgrade. Repeat. Badzone Run is a turn-based browser racer where every maneuver trades speed for control. Pilot a scrap-built vehicle through three procedural track sections against three AI rivals. Dodge hazards, shoot, ram, and survive to upgrade your next run. Free Runs and a shared daily challenge introduce tabletop ideas—facing, dice, risk and damage—through a compact digital game. No account required.

**Controls:** Mouse or touch. Choose speed, steering and action; inspect the movement preview, then confirm. Tab and Enter/Space operate the native controls. No real-time input is required.

**Objective:** Survive and cross the third section's finish line. Hull at zero means wrecked. Garage upgrades apply to Free Runs; the Daily uses a fixed loadout.
